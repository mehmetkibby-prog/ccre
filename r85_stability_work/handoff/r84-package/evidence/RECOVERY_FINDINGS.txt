PROVEN RACE
1. GlobalWorldCoordinator.recover completes owner recovery and sets
   core.recoveringWorld=false inside its nested serial operation.
2. Its outer recovery promise still occupies this.recovery until finally.
3. An independent late SharedCombatBridge.fail sets recoveringWorld=true.
4. recover sees the old promise and coalesces the late request into it.
5. Original completion clears this.recovery without another pass or retry.
Result: old recovery resolves, recoveringWorld=true, recovery=null, retry=null.
Both after-readiness-publication and after-serial-queue-resolution schedules
reproduce the state. The latter injection is independent of the flag setter.

CANDIDATE SCOPE
- Keep ingress fenced until recovery completion is published atomically.
- Track requests arriving after the owner-recovery pass has become ready.
- Coalesce multiple such requests into one subsequent ordinary recovery pass.
- Continue coalescing pre-ready duplicate requests as before.
- Pin the world row/incarnation after its boot and each channel's returned
  boot row/incarnation. Validate the exact rows before enabling world jobs and
  again after that ACK, immediately before marking this pass ready.
- Require all pinned owners current, running and not exited; channels must also
  have ready=true and matching online coordinator process records. A lost or
  changed generation throws through the existing fenced failure/retry policy.
- Preserve the existing replacement-failure fence and retry policy.
- If closing or closingWork has begun, never start follow-up owners and retain
  the ingress fence.
- Do not remove terminalIdle/global_world_busy guards, weaken epochs, expire
  leases, bypass durableExternal, or replay a gameplay mutation.
